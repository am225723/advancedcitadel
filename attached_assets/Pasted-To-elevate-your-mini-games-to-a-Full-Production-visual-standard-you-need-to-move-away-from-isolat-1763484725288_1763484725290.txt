4. Aesthetic Polish Checklist

To instantly make your existing scenes look better without downloading models:

Reflections: Set metalness={0.8} and roughness={0.2} on your car and engine parts. They will reflect the <Environment>.

Lighting: Add a colored PointLight (e.g., cyan or orange) near the focus area to create dramatic rim lighting.

Shadows: Enable shadows on the <Canvas> and add castShadow / receiveShadow to all meshes.

Bloom: The Bloom effect in the code above is crucial. It makes the "emissive" materials (like your Soul Ember and Spark Plugs) glow like neon lights.
