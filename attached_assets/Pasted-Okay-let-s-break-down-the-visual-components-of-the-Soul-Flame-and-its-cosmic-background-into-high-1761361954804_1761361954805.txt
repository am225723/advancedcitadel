Prompt 1: The Soul Flame (Core Visual Component)
"A highly detailed, photorealistic render of a volumetric 'soul flame.'
Flame Body:
 * Composition: The flame should be the central focus, roughly tear-drop or ovate in shape, but with a fluid, organic, and constantly shifting silhouette. It should appear to have significant volume and depth, not a flat 2D texture.
 * Internal Dynamics: Show internal currents and swirls of energy, as if it's a living entity. The base should be slightly wider and grounded, while the top tapers and extends upward.
 * Color Palette:
   * Core: The absolute center of the flame should be intensely bright, almost pure white-gold, radiating immense energy.
   * Mid-section: This transitions outwards to brilliant, energetic golds and vibrant oranges.
   * Edges: The outermost tendrils should cool slightly to deep, fiery oranges and hints of ethereal, translucent blue or purple at the very fringes, suggesting a magical or otherworldly quality rather than just heat.
 * Luminosity: The flame should be the primary light source in the image, casting a soft, warm, and radiant glow onto its immediate surroundings (though the background itself will be dark).
 * Transparency/Subtlety: The flame should have areas of semi-transparency, revealing glimpses of the cosmic background through its lighter, more ethereal parts.
 * Motion Blur/Flow: Render with subtle motion blur on its edges to convey constant, fluid movement, as if it's captured mid-flicker or pulse.
Particle Effects (Integrated):
 * Core Sparks: Small, intensely bright, star-like particles should be subtly visible within the brighter core of the flame, briefly appearing and fading.
 * Emanating Energy: Tiny, luminous motes of light (like glowing dust) should be gently drifting upwards and outwards from the main body of the flame, dissipating into the dark space above it. These should be sparse but present, contributing to the ethereal quality.
 * Absorbing Embers: Show subtle trails of tiny, glowing golden embers being drawn inward from the edges of the frame towards the flame's base, as if being absorbed. These trails should be delicate and barely visible, suggesting energy being gathered.
Style: High-resolution render, photorealistic but with an otherworldly, magical essence. Emphasize luminosity, depth, and dynamic visual flow. The flame should feel ancient, powerful, and deeply alive."
